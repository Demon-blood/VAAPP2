# v1.0.22 — Briefing Notification UX

## Problem

The backend already generates the full human-style VA briefing, but Android was presenting the
entire briefing as a normal notification `body`. Android collapses ordinary notification text,
which meant most of the briefing appeared hidden even though delivery itself was correct.

## Change

The Android background service now:

1. Keeps the complete briefing text.
2. Renders the complete text through `BigTextStyleInformation`.
3. Uses a concise collapsed body so the lock-screen / notification shade remains readable.
4. Adds paragraph spacing to the expanded briefing for easier scanning.
5. Applies the same behavior to both the current morning/afternoon/evening delivery path and
   the legacy one-daily-briefing fallback.
6. Leaves briefing delivery acknowledgements, durable delivery windows, retry behavior, and
   backend ownership semantics unchanged.

## Invariant

A successful notification display may advance the existing delivery-acknowledgement workflow,
but presentation changes must never change what the backend considers processed or delivered.

## Expected Android behavior

Collapsed:

    Evening VA briefing
    Everything is under control and nothing needs your attention right now… Expand to read the full briefing.

Expanded:

    Evening VA briefing
    Good evening.

    Everything is under control and nothing needs your attention right now.

    I cleared the routine mail and messages...

The full briefing remains available in the application as before.

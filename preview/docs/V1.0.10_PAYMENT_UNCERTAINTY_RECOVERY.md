# v1.0.10 — Payment Recovery & Human Boundary Integrity

v1.0.10 repairs a payment-creation uncertainty path that could incorrectly turn a provider/network defect into a human approval item.

## The boundary being repaired

A network interruption can happen after the bank receives a payment creation request but before VAAPP receives the provider response. Retrying the payment at that point can double-pay. The correct state is therefore **creation uncertain**: VAAPP must suppress retry, investigate provider state, and remain responsible for the exception.

Before v1.0.10, this path set `requires_user_action=true`, created a `payment_creation_uncertain` approval task, and the bill lifecycle could describe the state as `authorization_required` even when there was no bank authorization URL. That violated the product rule that provider/system defects stay VA-owned.

## v1.0.10 behavior

- `creation_uncertain` is VA-owned and never becomes Needs You merely because the provider response was lost.
- The uncertain payment remains an active payment, preserving the existing anti-double-payment guard. No automatic second payment is created while outcome is unknown.
- The banking Autopilot reconciles uncertain creations against booked transactions from the exact source bank account.
- Recovery requires exactly one transaction matching amount, currency, booking-date proximity, and strong creditor/reference evidence.
- A recovered payment stores additive `PaymentRecoveryEvidence`, marks the payment complete, and marks the bill paid.
- Zero matches remain `waiting_for_evidence`; multiple matches remain `ambiguous`. Both stay VA-owned and do not trigger an automatic payment retry.
- Historical `payment_creation_uncertain` tasks are closed by the recovery path so old fake human-boundary cards do not linger.
- Operational Guardian counts unresolved uncertain creation as a **system issue**, not as Needs You.
- Genuine bank SCA/authorization remains human-required only when a real provider authorization URL exists.

## Evidence contract

A local payment intent, missing HTTP response, or generated recovery attempt is never completion evidence. Terminal recovery requires one independent booked bank transaction with a provider transaction identifier. Ambiguous evidence does not complete the payment.

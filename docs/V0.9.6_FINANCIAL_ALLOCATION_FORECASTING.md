# Full-Time VA v0.9.6 — Financial Allocation & Forecasting

Phase 7 turns the existing Financial Autopilot into a source-backed forward cash planner. It does not replace provider evidence, payment policy, or bank-side verification. Forecasting determines what cash can safely be treated as surplus; actual own-account movements still use the existing durable Enable Banking transfer executor and remain incomplete until provider state is reconciled.

## Forecast evidence model

The forecast uses only evidence already owned by VAAPP:

- effective connected-account balances after locally committed payments/transfers;
- per-account safety reserves and Financial Autopilot floors;
- learned recurring cashflows with evidence-derived confidence;
- exact open bills and their due dates when available;
- explicit/learned budget envelopes;
- learned Revolut investment funding requirements;
- current-month income and configured tax allocation percentages.

It produces a base and a conservative scenario over 30–180 days. The default horizon is 90 days. The conservative scenario discounts uncertain recurring income and increases variable/recurring debit assumptions. Unknown income is not invented.

## Protected cash and investable/sweepable surplus

Cash is allocatable only when the conservative scenario remains above protected floors. Protected floors include account safety reserves, configured account target floors, and the global minimum operating-cash floor. Expected investment funding is modeled as a protected outflow, not as available surplus.

This preserves the required cash structure:

- Personal and Pro scopes never rebalance silently across scopes.
- Revolut Pro never directly funds personal Kraken.
- Safety/emergency reserve is never investable.
- Revolut native scheduled investment contributions remain the execution mechanism; VAAPP protects/prefunds the cash they require.
- Kraken funding/trading remains separately policy-gated and disabled until explicitly configured.
- Kraken withdrawals remain disabled/not implemented.

## Durable ledgers

Phase 7 adds three additive tables:

- `FinancialForecastRun` — encrypted exact forecast snapshot plus input fingerprint and conservative/base minima.
- `FinancialAllocationPlan` — per-scope protected floor, projected minima and forecast-safe surplus.
- `FinancialAllocationAction` — durable same-scope transfer intent linked to the real `OwnAccountTransfer` provider ledger.

The forecast snapshot and allocation-plan details are encrypted at rest. Existing bank/account balances remain in their pre-existing finance tables.

## Allocation policy

For a forecast-safe surplus, destination priority is:

1. controlled spending-wallet prefunding when a configured/learned target has a gap;
2. current-month tax contribution gap;
3. reserve target/floor gap;
4. ordinary savings for remaining surplus.

Only an `operating` account with payment execution enabled and `May send own-account transfers` may be a source. Destinations must be in the same Personal/Pro scope and explicitly accept surplus. Existing per-transfer, daily, monthly, minimum-transfer, currency, active-transfer and reserve checks remain authoritative inside `create_own_account_transfer`.

## Ambiguity and idempotency

A `FinancialAllocationAction` is persisted as `dispatching` before provider execution. The underlying own-account transfer itself persists its intent before the provider POST. Forecast-run scoped idempotency keys prevent duplicate replay inside a run. On recovery, allocation actions reconcile against the durable transfer ledger before any new action is created.

`creation_uncertain` is never treated as success and is never blindly retried. Bank SCA remains `needs_user_auth` and is surfaced with the provider authorization URL.

## Automation

The existing `banking.autopilot` durable workflow now runs the forecast/allocation cycle after account/transaction/payment/transfer reconciliation. Unchanged inputs reuse the recent forecast rather than creating repeated allocation work. The manual Financial Autopilot run forces a fresh forecast after synchronizing current financial evidence.

The legacy `run_budget_autopilot` function remains for compatibility/tests, but scheduled and manual product execution is superseded by the forecast-aware allocator.

## API / Android

New authenticated APIs:

- `GET /api/finance/forecast`
- `POST /api/finance/forecast/run?horizon_days=90`

Android adds **Money → Forecast**. It shows Personal/Pro starting managed cash, protected floor, base/conservative minima, safe allocatable surplus, forecast checkpoints, investment-funding protection, and the durable allocation actions. Provider authorization remains accessible when bank SCA is required.

## Completion contract

A forecast is evidence and a plan is intent. Neither means money moved. An allocation action reaches verified completion only when its linked `OwnAccountTransfer` reaches the bank-verified completed state.

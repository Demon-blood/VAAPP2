# v1.0.6 — Autonomous Executive Loop & Commitment Graph

VAAPP treats every durable `VAObjective` as a commitment it owns until the requested real-world outcome is independently verified.

The commitment contract is:

`observe → own → prioritize → execute → wait/follow up → verify → close`

## Commitment semantics

`VAObjective.goal` is the desired outcome. `VAObjectiveStep` is the execution plan. `VAFollowUp` is the persisted follow-through schedule. `VAOutcomeEvidence` is the proof required before closure. `VACommitmentEdge` adds durable dependency and continuation relationships between objective nodes without duplicating objective state.

Ownership and waiting are deliberately separate. A commitment may be waiting on a provider, counterparty, authentication step, or internal capability while still being owned by the VA. `needs_user` is reserved for a genuine human boundary; provider/system failures remain VA-owned internal work.

## Executive queue

The Work → Operations view is projected into five employer-facing groups:

- **Needs You** — only unavoidable authentication, physical action, or consequential judgment.
- **Working now** — commitments being executed or verified.
- **Waiting / following up** — provider/counterparty waits that remain owned by the VA, with the next check/follow-up already scheduled when available.
- **VA is resolving** — internal capability/system blockers. These do not become user tasks.
- **Recently finished** — outcomes closed after the required evidence was observed.

The raw recent-objective stream is no longer the primary Work presentation.

## Prioritization

Ready objective steps are ordered by commitment priority first, then due date, then scheduled execution time. The public commitment projection also exposes a rank score that accounts for priority, risk, deadline proximity, lifecycle stage, and stale open work.

## Graph

`VACommitmentEdge` persists known objective relationships discovered from objective context/plan fields such as `prior_objective_id`, `parent_objective_id`, `depends_on_objective_id`, `blocked_by_objective_id`, and `dependency_objective_ids`.

The graph is reconciled in the autonomous core cycle and is idempotent.

## Verification invariant

The release does not weaken the existing completion rule. Starting, clicking, sending, booking, paying, or submitting is not completion. A commitment closes only when its objective/step verifier or source reconciliation records the required terminal evidence.

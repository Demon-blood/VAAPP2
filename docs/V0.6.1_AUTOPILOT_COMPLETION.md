# Full-Time VA v0.6.1 — Autopilot Completion

v0.6.1 is the production hardening layer on top of v0.6.0 Daily Intelligence. It keeps all v0.5.1 financial-document safeguards and v0.5.2 Gmail conflict recovery intact while closing the remaining gaps between “automation” and a genuinely unattended full-time VA.

## What changed

### Proactive planner

A durable `autopilot.plan` job is enqueued every 15 minutes and is also appended to **Run VA** after Gmail, banking, contacts, connectors and housekeeping complete. It:

- finds validated bills inside the configured auto-pay horizon and idempotently enqueues `bill.lifecycle`;
- never creates a second payment when an active payment already exists;
- revisits legacy queued email replies and automatically sends them when the deterministic reply safety policy says they are low risk;
- escalates a due generic task only when no deterministic executor exists, making the user interaction an actual ambiguity/physical-action exception rather than a routine approval;
- reconciles the action queue after planning.

### Reply autonomy and Personal Ops Profile

Routine, short, non-sensitive replies can now be sent automatically even without a hand-authored reply rule. The safety gate blocks unattended replies involving financial documents, banking, legal/security/identity/medical contexts, authorization, contracts, payments, credentials, SCA/Itsme, or other commitment-sensitive content.

Successful reply handling learns deterministic sender preferences in the existing `operation_preferences` table. Learned preferences require repeated matching outcomes before they are enabled. Explicit preferences continue to take precedence. Security, credential, bank-authorization, payment-authorization and legal decisions are never learned into unattended shortcuts.

### Provider self-healing

Terminal workflow failures are classified as:

- `transient` — rate limits, 408/409/425/429, provider 5xx, timeouts, connection resets, DNS/service-unavailable errors and expired leases;
- `user_required` — OAuth/credential/permission problems, 401/403, SCA/Itsme, bank authorization and security challenges;
- `unknown` — not safe to retry forever without inspection.

Provider health automatically requeues only transient dead letters, after a cooldown, with a bounded recovery budget. Transient failures stay out of **Needs you** while that recovery budget is available. OAuth/SCA/security/configuration failures are never auto-bypassed.

### Single durable payment path

Scheduled banking jobs now synchronize accounts, reconcile receipts and refresh payment statuses. New payment initiation is delegated to the durable `bill.lifecycle` jobs owned by the proactive planner. This removes the previous duplicate best-effort auto-pay path that could catch an initiation failure without giving the workflow engine a retryable failure.

### Exception-only side effects

Google Calendar, Drive archival and scheduled connector failures no longer automatically become “approve this” tasks when the underlying failure is transient or provider-auth related. Those failures are allowed to reach the durable job engine, which retries them and uses provider recovery. A user-facing task is created only when the failure is deterministic/ambiguous and Autopilot cannot infer a safe repair.

### Daily Intelligence and Today

- **Needs you** remains ahead of the Daily Briefing.
- Raw email/bill/task/payment counters were removed from Today; a count-free Work Library remains for optional inspection.
- Autopilot health distinguishes `self-healing`, actionable recovery failures, and provider setup requirements.
- Core Google, primary AI and banking setup/auth requirements are surfaced in **Needs you**.
- The Daily Briefing includes a chronological **VA activity timeline** in addition to the aggregate “What the VA did” section.
- Exception notifications include provider authorization and funding blockers and remain deduplicated.
- Daily briefing scheduling follows the runtime-configured local delivery hour and has one durable idempotency key per local day, so process restarts do not shift the briefing clock.

## Safety boundaries preserved

v0.6.1 does not bypass bank SCA/Itsme, creditor/IBAN approval, safety reserves, provider OAuth consent, security challenges, or ambiguous legal/financial decisions. It also does not invent a generic executor for physical/manual tasks: when a due task genuinely cannot be performed through a connected provider, it becomes a **Needs you** exception rather than being falsely marked complete.

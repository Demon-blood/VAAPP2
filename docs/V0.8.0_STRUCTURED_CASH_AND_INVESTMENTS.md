# Full-Time VA v0.8.0 — Structured cash and investments

This release separates operating cash, spending cash, protected reserves and investments.

- Beobank Personal is the operating/bills source.
- Revolut Personal is a spending role. Financial Autopilot learns a one-week cash target and includes expected Revolut Securities portfolio funding. Revolut remains the scheduler/executor of its own portfolio contributions.
- Revolut Pro is a Pro operating account. It is not automatically drained into Personal.
- Recurring obligations are learned from transaction identity, direction, description, amount and monthly recurrence. Child support, housing contributions, subscriptions, utilities and insurance protect cash before surplus movement.
- Revolut Securities Account Statement and P&L XLSX/PDF imports create Brokerage/Robo portfolio views, positions, trades, dividends, withholding tax and realised P&L. Robo is detected by Robo management fee activity.
- Kraken can optionally receive Personal investment funding through Enable Banking PIS to a configured verified EUR deposit destination. Deposit reconciliation uses the Kraken EUR balance and only one active funding intent at a time. Optional policy-bound Spot purchases use a Kraken API key with query-funds and modify-trades permissions. No withdrawal endpoint is implemented.
- Cross-scope transfers remain excluded from ordinary budgets but are labelled owner draw / owner contribution where connected account scopes make the direction explicit.

Provider execution is fail-closed: uncertain payment creation is never blindly retried, bank SCA is surfaced when required, and Kraken trading is off until explicitly enabled.

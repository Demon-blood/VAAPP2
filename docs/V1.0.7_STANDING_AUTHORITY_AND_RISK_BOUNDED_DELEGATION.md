# v1.0.7 — Standing Authority & Risk-Bounded Delegation

v1.0.7 reduces repeated approval work without weakening the human-boundary rules established in v1.0.5–v1.0.6.

## Product contract

A user may grant the VA **standing authority** for a predefined scope. That authority is explicit, revocable, auditable, rate-limited, risk-limited, and—where money is involved—amount-limited.

Standing authority is not completion evidence. Every real-world objective remains open until its normal provider/source postcondition is independently verified.

## Initial scopes

- **Routine communications** — ordinary email/SMS replies and follow-ups.
- **Bounded portal transactions** — ordinary purchases, returns, refunds, and cancellations when the action is not a hard human boundary and any monetary amount is known and within the configured EUR cap.

Each scope is disabled until explicitly enabled. The stored policy uses the existing `OperationPreference` table with `source=explicit`; v1.0.7 does not infer standing authority from prior behavior.

Ordinary calendar create/update/cancel work is already autonomous under the existing calendar policy, so v1.0.7 does not add a cosmetic calendar-authority toggle. Calendar conflicts and critical-risk changes keep their existing human boundary.

## Hard human boundaries

Standing authority can never waive fresh account-holder/provider participation for:

- bank/provider strong customer authentication;
- OTP, passcodes, passwords, or other credentials;
- identity proof or identity verification;
- signatures, contract acceptance, or agreeing to terms;
- medical/legal consent;
- account/security changes such as password changes or account deletion;
- bank transfers/withdrawals that require account-holder authorization;
- physical-world actions the software cannot perform.

Critical-risk actions always remain specific human decisions.

## Risk controls

Every policy has a maximum risk ceiling and a daily action count. Portal transactions additionally require an explicit EUR cap. Monetary browser actions with an unknown amount fail closed and remain specific decisions.

Optional counterparty allowlists and expiry timestamps can narrow an authority further.

## Execution behavior

When an objective is in `needs_user` only because an executable email/SMS proposal needs a material decision, the autonomous core re-evaluates it against standing authority. If the exact proposal fits the configured policy, the VA resumes the durable executor automatically and records a `va_standing_authority_used` audit event.

Material browser operations may similarly resume only when the persisted operation fits the bounded portal policy. Existing provider verification remains mandatory before objective completion.

## Android

Work → Operations exposes the standing-authority scopes and their active limits. The UI explicitly states that hard human boundaries always stay with the user.

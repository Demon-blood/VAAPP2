# Full-Time VA v0.7.0 — Communications & Financial Autopilot

v0.7.0 turns the existing email/bill Autopilot into a unified communications and personal-finance operations layer. It is designed for unattended execution, while keeping provider-mandated authorization and high-impact ambiguity explicit.

## Gmail lifecycle

The VA now separates **classification** from **Inbox state**. Adding a label no longer implies that the message should stay in Inbox.

- Routine normal/low-priority categorized mail is archived by removing Gmail's `INBOX` label.
- Action-required, high/urgent, protected legal/health/security/family mail and unsent reply candidates stay in Inbox.
- Paid receipts and informational financial notices are retained but filed out of Inbox.
- A one-time v0.7.0 reconciliation reuses persisted decisions to archive safely classified messages left in Inbox by earlier releases. It does not spend AI tokens and never replays historical trash decisions.
- A second-stage cleanup may move **read**, already-processed, low-priority newsletter/social/notification mail to Gmail Trash after a configurable grace period (14 days by default). Unread, protected, financial, task, bill, reply, order, subscription, support or calendar mail is excluded.

## Communications Autopilot

### SMS

When Android grants the required permissions and SMS role, Full-Time VA can ingest SMS history, receive new SMS, persist received/sent messages as the default handler, send multipart SMS, classify conversations, create follow-up tasks, and automatically send low-risk replies.

Sensitive content is deterministically protected after AI output. Bank/payment/IBAN, legal, identity/security/OTP, and medical content cannot be auto-replied to by a model decision.

### WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and Messenger

The native notification listener ingests visible message notifications from supported package names. When the original app exposes Android `RemoteInput`, Full-Time VA can execute a safe direct reply through that notification action.

Notification refreshes are deduplicated using a content fingerprint and group-summary notifications are ignored. Hidden notifications such as “New message” are retained for attention and never auto-replied to.

This is intentionally not represented as unrestricted access to another app's private database. If a messaging provider does not expose message content or an action through Android, Full-Time VA does not fabricate that access.

### Calls

The app can request Android's Call Screening role. The screening decision is made locally so the phone can respond within Android's short call-screening deadline:

- explicit VIP/allow rules;
- explicit silence rules;
- explicit block rules;
- optional silence-unknown policy;
- known contacts are recognized locally when Contacts permission is granted.

Call outcomes and recent call history feed the unified backend communications timeline and Daily Briefing.

Full-Time VA does **not** claim to capture or converse over both sides of ordinary cellular call audio. A future spoken AI receptionist requires a carrier-forwarding/VoIP provider integration rather than bypassing Android's protected call-audio model.

### Native credential security

The background Android services need the paired backend token even when Flutter is not running. v0.7.0 encrypts that native copy with an AES-GCM key generated inside Android Keystore. Previous plaintext preference keys are deleted when credentials are synchronized.

## Financial Autopilot

### Effective cash safety

Every irreversible bill payment and own-account transfer is authorized against an **effective available balance**, not just the last cached AIS balance. The backend subtracts locally committed bill payments and source-account transfers that may not yet be reflected by the bank balance. Completed movements remain reserved until a newer bank balance sync has occurred. Budget target planning also counts transfers already on the way to a destination so multiple operating accounts cannot over-fund the same reserve/savings target while bank balances are catching up.

Source bank-account rows are locked at the execution boundary, so concurrent payments/transfers from the same account are serialized before the reserve check and provider POST.

A separate global operating-account floor defaults to **EUR 1,000** and is enforced again at transfer execution. It is configurable, but the VA will not automatically sweep an operating account below it. A one-minute watchdog also quarantines any money-movement intent left in `creating` for 15 minutes after a process interruption; it becomes `creation_uncertain`, remains cash-reserved, and cannot be retried automatically.


v0.7.0 extends the existing bill-payment safety architecture rather than creating a separate unguarded payment path.

### Transaction intelligence and budgets

- bank transaction history is persisted and categorized;
- internal transfers are identified using the actual counterparty side of the transaction;
- incoming salary/external credits are not misclassified simply because the creditor side is the user's own connected account;
- monthly spend is forecast from recent transaction history;
- budget envelopes can use explicit limits or learned limits;
- envelope reserve targets contribute to the cash-safety plan;
- the tax envelope can reserve a configurable percentage of current-month external income, e.g. 15%;
- upcoming validated obligations participate in the retained-cash calculation;
- the finance overview reports spending, effective budget, remaining budget, overspending, income, obligations and active own-account transfers.

### Own-account transfers

Automatic rebalancing is only allowed between connected accounts in the same account scope and currency. The source account must also be explicitly enabled for payment execution and automatic internal transfers.

Safety is enforced twice: during planning and again immediately before provider submission.

- explicit source and destination IBANs;
- source-account row lock before creating the money-movement intent;
- idempotency key unique in the database;
- one active transfer per source at a time;
- source safety reserve and policy floor;
- global maximum per transfer;
- global daily internal-transfer limit;
- per-account monthly outbound limit;
- minimum transfer amount;
- general surplus routes to savings, while tax/reserve accounts receive funds only for an explicit target or tax-allocation gap.

The local transfer intent and OAuth state are committed **before** the irreversible provider payment-creation POST. If the network drops after submission, the result becomes `creation_uncertain`; automatic creation retry is suppressed and the user is asked to check the bank. This prevents a timeout from becoming a duplicate transfer.

### Existing bill payment path hardened

Bill payment creation now takes a database row lock on the bill and commits its local Payment intent before the provider POST. Concurrent workers therefore cannot both submit the same bill. A network-ambiguous creation becomes `creation_uncertain` and cannot be blindly retried.

The source account IBAN is explicitly passed to payment initiation. Existing creditor allowlists, exact IBAN checks, creditor maximum amount, account scope checks and account safety reserves remain in force.

### SCA / bank authorization

The VA may make the decision and initiate an eligible payment/transfer, but it does not bypass bank-required SCA. When a bank returns an authorization URL, it appears in Needs You. On a successful signed callback, the authorization URL and Needs You state are cleared even if the bank is still processing the payment.

## Daily Intelligence

The existing Daily Briefing now includes:

- received calls and messages;
- protected/actionable communication exceptions;
- communication replies sent by the VA;
- own-account transfer activity and authorization/reconciliation exceptions;
- the existing email, bills, payments, tasks, calendar, documents, provider health and activity timeline.

## Database/deployment strategy

All v0.7.0 persistence is implemented with **new tables** (`bank_transactions`, `budget_envelopes`, `bank_autopilot_policies`, `own_account_transfers`, `communication_events`, `communication_actions`, `communication_rules`). Existing live tables are not altered. This is intentional because the application currently initializes schema with SQLAlchemy `metadata.create_all()`, which safely creates missing tables but is not a column-migration system.

## Android one-time setup

After installing v0.7.0, open **Services → Communications Autopilot** and complete the Android prompts for the capabilities you want the VA to use:

1. SMS/read-call-log/contacts runtime permissions.
2. Default SMS role for full SMS handling.
3. Notification access for supported messaging-app notifications/direct replies.
4. Call Screening role.

These are Android security boundaries and cannot be granted silently by the app. Once granted, normal handling is unattended.

## Known platform boundary

MMS role eligibility is present and MMS arrival is recorded, but v0.7.0 does not decode arbitrary MMS attachment/body payloads inside the native receiver. It records the event without taking an unsafe action. SMS text management is fully implemented; richer MMS parsing should be treated as a separate provider/content-decoding feature.
